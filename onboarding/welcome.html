🇰🇷 KOREAN OCR SUBTITLE EXTRACTOR

You're installed. Here's how to use it.

Three steps, on any YouTube video with Korean captions baked into the picture.

1

🎯 Draw a box around the captions

Open a YouTube video, play it until a caption is visible, then click Set Subtitle Region in the small panel that appears near the top-right of the page. Drag a box tightly around the caption text.

2

▶ Click Start

This presses play for you and speeds through the video (up to 4x) while it reads the captions. The first time in a browser session, it takes a moment to load the OCR engine — that's normal, and it's all local, nothing is uploaded anywhere.

3

⬇ Export when it's done

Click Export .srt to download a standard subtitle file with everything it captured, timed to match the video.

Try it on YouTube now →

Everything runs locally in your browser — no server, no account, no API key, nothing uploaded. Pin the extension icon (puzzle piece in your toolbar → pin) for one-click access to the same controls without hunting for the on-page panel.
